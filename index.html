About CartShare

Dorm mates, office teams, travel groups and families often coordinate purchases through chats and spreadsheets. That makes it hard to hit minimum order values for free delivery and hard to know who added what. CartShare puts everyone in one room with one cart and a clear record of every change.

How It Works

1

Create or join a room

Start a room and share its six-character code with your group.

2

Fill the cart together

Everyone adds items. Each item shows who added it and when.

3

Unlock free delivery

Watch the progress bar reach ₹500, then print the receipt.
